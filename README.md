TallyUP : A personal finance tracker

TallyUP is a personal expense tracking and financial management application designed to help users monitor, organize, and understand their daily spending habits.


Managing everyday expenses can become difficult when multiple small transactions are made throughout the day. TallyUP provides a simple and convenient platform where users can record their expenses, categorize their spending, and analyze their financial activity through visual statistics.

The application provides a mobile-friendly and user-focused interface, with a dark theme, clear cards, rounded input fields, green action buttons, and simple navigation, making it convenient to manage expenses across smartphones and other devices.
